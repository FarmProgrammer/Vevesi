**Quest Type**: Exploration/Ritual  
**Objective**:  
A spring high in the mountains has dried up, threatening the valley’s water supply. Local legend says the spring was a gift from the Thalassa . The players must journey to the source to appease her.

- **Twist**: The spring has been blocked by miners who unearthed something unnatural—a strange construct, its arcane runes still glowing above a vein of rare gems.. 10 minutes after the players arrive, unless they stop the miners, they will use gunpowder to loosen the earth and get to the minerals, unintentionally setting the construct free. It is a giant animated statue. If the players take the statue below 30hp it will begin to limp in the direction of [[The Ghostheart Mechanism]] to get repaired, the players can finish it as it moves or follow to see where it goes discovering the ancient vault.
- Alberic Ironbeard: The foreman, a Kobold from valerius having spent the last of his money on men and supplies for this trip in a bid to stay relevant as many of the noble classes of Valerius are being torn down in favour of the emperor's new method of rule. Failure will mean poverty and debt for him and his family.
- Jernau Olirouge: A centaur from Jupiter sent to oversee the investment and progress of Alberic. Wanting profit for the investors even at the cost of polluting the spring.
- Construct Encounter:
	- Rage: The construct is there to protect the crystals as a potential important point during the war 10000 years ago but has been long forgotten, still running on its old instructions, if the players destroy a crystal the construct will gain a +2 to hit but lose its hardness for that round.
	- Brittle Hardness: The construct repairs its outer shell as it fights, but significant damage will leave its internals vulnerable for some time, if it takes over 20 damage from 1 source attacks from that same direction get a +5 status bonus to hit and hardness does not apply until the end of the round.
- **Reward**: If the miners survive they offer what crystals they salvaged in the worth of 150 gp and leave the players with a few of their supplies and treasures:
